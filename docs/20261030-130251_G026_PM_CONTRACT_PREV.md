Restart during active round confirms; cancel never resets state. Help/theme opens pause; closing does not automatically resume. Blur/visibilityhidden/pagehide while a modal is open preserves modal and sets protection-pause precedence for its return.

Escape cancels/close, never confirms an irreversible action. Native dialog cancel/close followups must not steal focus after explicit resume.
